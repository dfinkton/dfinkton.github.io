## What it's for
Watching without acting — and the discovery that watching alone makes things worse.

## Practices
Tracking one feeling arrival to departure. Logging patterns across weeks. Notice-and-allow, not notice-and-fix. What to do during the rough patch.

## Studies
- Lindsay, E. K., & Creswell, J. D. (2017). Mechanisms of mindfulness training: Monitor and Acceptance Theory. *Clinical Psychology Review, 51*, 48–59. — Two tenets: monitoring increases awareness *and* affective reactivity; acceptance regulates the affective response.
- Lindsay, E. K., Young, S., Smyth, J. M., Brown, K. W., & Creswell, J. D. (2018). Acceptance lowers stress reactivity. *Psychoneuroendocrinology, 87*, 63–73. — 153 stressed adults, three structurally equivalent 15-lesson arms; Monitor+Accept beat both Monitor Only and active control on biological stress reactivity.
- Lindsay, E. K., et al. (2019). Mindfulness training reduces loneliness and increases social contact. *PNAS, 116*(9), 3488–3493.
- The 2024 *Stress* paper testing MAT against ReSource data: early attentional and interoceptive training may enhance stress sensitivity and increase stress-induced cortisol.
- The critical review of MAT in *Mindfulness* (2023), identifying four problems in its supporting literature.

## Central teaching
Expect a period where you feel worse. That is the first half working while the second half is missing.
