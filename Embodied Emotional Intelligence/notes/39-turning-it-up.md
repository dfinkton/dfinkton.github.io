## Key points
Deliberately raising a feeling when its energy is what the moment needs.

## Correction to an earlier note in this guide
Previously recorded that essentially no research exists on deliberate upregulation. That was wrong, and it was the same failure pattern that recurs in these notes — a universal negative asserted without a targeted search.

## Studies
Ochsner and colleagues, *For better or for worse: neural systems supporting the cognitive down- and up-regulation of negative emotion* (*NeuroImage*, 2004), found that **both up- and down-regulation recruited prefrontal and anterior cingulate control regions, with amygdala activity modulated up or down in line with the regulatory goal.** Up-regulation uniquely recruited left rostromedial PFC, implicated in the retrieval of emotion knowledge; down-regulation uniquely recruited right lateral and orbital PFC, implicated in behavioural inhibition. Self-focused regulation recruited medial prefrontal regions; situation-focused regulation recruited lateral ones.

So deliberate upregulation is a real, separable capacity with its own neural signature — not downregulation run backwards. What is still missing is a *practice protocol* and any outcome evidence for training it.

## Still to research
Whether anyone has trained upregulation of adaptive emotion and measured a result.
