## Studies
Klimecki, Leiberg, Ricard & Singer (2014), *SCAN, 9*(6), 873–879 — empathy training increased negative affect and anterior insula / anterior midcingulate activation; subsequent compassion training reversed it and produced positive affect via a **non-overlapping** network (ventral striatum, pregenual ACC, medial OFC). · Klimecki et al. (2013), *Cerebral Cortex, 23*(7), 1552–1561. · Leiberg, Klimecki & Singer (2011), *PLOS ONE, 6*, e17798 — behavioral prosocial outcome. · Klimecki & Singer (2012), empathic distress fatigue rather than compassion fatigue. · Klimecki, Vuilleumier & Sander (2016), the Inequality Game. · Lutz et al. (2008), *PLOS ONE, 3*, e1897 — experts showed *greater* insula, amygdala, right TPJ and right pSTS response to distress. Self-compassion: Neff — **verify, note the measurement debate.**
