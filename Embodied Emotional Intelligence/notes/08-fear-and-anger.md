## Practices
Graduated exposure starting at a 3. Substitution — anger's instruction is *defend the boundary*; escalation is one vehicle among several.

## One thing to make explicit: avoidance hides inside apparent approach
You can go to the party and spend it on your phone. You can have the hard conversation while keeping it abstract enough that nothing lands. The rep only counts if you let the thing reach you — the same concrete-versus-abstract distinction that runs through lessons 6 and 9.

## Studies
Exposure and habituation are the strongest mechanistic evidence in clinical psychology. PTSD meta-analyses: exposure-based treatments keep improving after treatment ends (d ≈ 0.27).
