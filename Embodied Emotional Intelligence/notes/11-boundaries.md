## Key distinction
The distinction that makes the essay useful: a boundary is what *you* will do; a demand is what *someone else* must do. Most people who think they are setting boundaries are issuing demands. The emotional work is tolerating someone's displeasure without folding or escalating. DBT interpersonal effectiveness is the closest clinical analogue and the weakest-evidenced DBT module — say so.
