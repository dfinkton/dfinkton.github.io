## Scope
Hot versus cold, and the DMN. Decentering goes to lesson 7; abstract-versus-concrete to lesson 6; the forward-facing version to lesson 9.

## What it's for
Hot, cold, flooded, wandering — and which practices are available in which state.

## Practices
Identifying your flooded signature. The delay sentence. Returning from wandering. Working with rumination.

## Framing
The network that ruminates is the network that plans and reads other minds. Control over entry and exit, not silence.
