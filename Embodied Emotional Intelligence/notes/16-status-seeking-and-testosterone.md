## Studies
Testosterone tracks status-seeking, not aggression — Archer (2006), *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews, 30*, 319–345, finds the aggression correlation weak. It rises with competition and falls with caregiving: Gettler, McDade, Feranil & Kuzawa (2011), *PNAS, 108*(39), 16194–16199 — 624 Filipino men, becoming a partnered father produced steep declines. Grebe et al. (2019), *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews, 98*, 221–233 — meta-analytic support. Wingfield et al. (1990) challenge hypothesis. Pair with Gardner & Steinberg on peer presence.
