## Studies
Kellam et al. (2008), *Drug and Alcohol Dependence, 95*(Suppl 1), 5–28 — Good Behavior Game. Baltimore, 41 classrooms, 19 schools. At ages 19–21: lower rates of drug and alcohol use disorders, regular smoking, antisocial personality disorder, delinquency and incarceration for violent crime, suicidal ideation. **It taught no emotion content.** Effects larger where teachers were freshly trained and monitored; concentrated in boys who entered first grade already aggressive. · Sampson, Raudenbush & Earls (1997), *Science, 277*, 918–924 — **observational; no intervention evidence. State plainly.**
